About PCDF

The Voice of Reason,
The Power of Words

The Port City International University Debating Forum (PCIU Debating Forum) is the premier debating society of Port City International University. Founded in 2023, PCDF exists to cultivate a culture of critical thinking, articulate expression, and respectful discourse on campus. We bring together students from every discipline to train, compete, and grow as speakers, thinkers, and leaders.

Our Goals

  • 01 Build a thriving community of debaters who value logic, evidence, and open-minded inquiry.
  • 02 Represent Port City International University with distinction at national and international tournaments.
  • 03 Promote democratic, fact-based dialogue on the issues that shape our campus and society.
  • 04 Empower every member—novice or veteran—to find and refine their authentic voice.

Our Objectives

  • + Conduct weekly training, workshops, and internal debate sessions throughout the academic year.
  • + Organize flagship events such as the PCDF Nationals and inter-university championships.
  • + Publish reports, motion banks, and resources that benefit the wider debate community.
  • + Mentor members into confident speakers, adjudicators, and future committee leaders.

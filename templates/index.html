About PCDF

PCIU Debating Forum

PCIU Debating Forum (PCDF) is the premier student-led debating organization of Port City International University (PCIU) dedicated to developing critical thinking, public speaking, leadership, research, and constructive dialogue among young people.

Through debate competitions, workshops, public speaking sessions, training programs, and various intellectual activities, PCDF creates a platform where students can think critically, speak confidently, and engage meaningfully with society.

About PCDF

The Voice of Reason,
The Power of Words

The Port City International University Debating Forum (PCIU Debating Forum) is the premier debating society of Port City International University. Founded in 2023, PCDF exists to cultivate a culture of critical thinking, articulate expression, and respectful discourse on campus. We bring together students from every discipline to train, compete, and grow as speakers, thinkers, and leaders.

Auto-Swap (5s)

Strategic Vision & Goals

  • 01 Build a thriving community valuing logic, empirical evidence, and open-minded inquiry.
  • 02 Represent Port City International University with distinction at national championships.
  • 03 Promote democratic, fact-based dialogue on issues shaping campus and society.
  • 04 Empower every member—novice or veteran—to refine their authentic oratorical voice.

Operational Objectives

  • 01 Conduct weekly training masterclasses, workshops, and internal sparring debates.
  • 02 Organize flagship tournaments including PCDF Nationals and inter-varsity cups.
  • 03 Publish annual motions, debate research reports, and motion bank archives.
  • 04 Mentor members into championship adjudicators and future student leaders.
